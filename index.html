One secure workspace for your files, notes and team

MART Drive is a cloud storage and collaboration web app. Upload files to your own Google Drive, organize them in folders, share them with your team or organization, and keep notes and web links with live previews, all in one place.

What you can do with MART Drive

Built for individuals, clubs, schools and organizations that need a simple, shared place to keep their work.

cloud_upload

File storage

Upload documents, images, videos and PDFs. Files are stored in your own Google Drive, and you can preview them in the app.

groups

Organizations & teams

Create an organization with one or more owners, add members by email or invite link, and choose whether join requests are approved automatically or by an owner. Everyone in it can view, comment on and edit the shared files, notes and links.

link

Notes & links with preview

Save text notes and web links. Links show a live preview with title, description and image so they are easy to recognize.

folder

Folders & organization

Use folders, select multiple items at once, star important items, move things around, search everything, see your storage usage, and restore anything from the trash.

lock

Access control

Share individual files with specific people as viewer, commenter or editor, and track them in Shared by me and Shared from. Personal items stay private to you.

devices

Works everywhere

A fast, responsive web app for phone, tablet and desktop, with light and dark themes. Nothing to install.

How MART Drive uses your Google data

We keep this simple and transparent.

  • Your profile: your name, email address and profile photo are used only to sign you in, show who you are, and match you to organizations you belong to.
  • Google Drive access: MART Drive requests the limited drive.file permission. It can only create and manage files that you upload through MART Drive. It cannot see or change the rest of your Google Drive. It also reads your Drive storage quota (used and free space) so it can show you how much space is left.
  • App data: folder names, notes, saved links, sharing details, organization membership and join requests are stored in Google Firebase (Firestore) so they sync across your devices. Organization logos you upload are hosted on ImgBB.
  • Your control: we never sell your data or use it for advertising. You can delete items in the app and revoke access any time from your Google Account permissions.

1. Introduction

Welcome to MART Drive ("we", "our", "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our web application hosted at https://rovermdashfaq.github.io/mart-drive (the "Service").

MART Drive is a cloud storage management tool that helps you upload and manage files directly in your own Google Drive account. We take your privacy seriously and are committed to transparency about the data we handle.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to provide the Service:

  • Google Authentication Data: When you sign in with Google, we receive basic profile information (such as your name, email address, and profile picture) and an authentication token. This is handled securely by Google Firebase.
  • Google Drive File Metadata: When you upload or manage files, we store metadata such as the file name, Google Drive file ID, file size, and MIME type. This metadata is stored in Firebase Firestore to help us display your file list and manage your account.
  • No File Contents Stored on Our Servers: We do not store the actual contents of your files on our servers. Your files remain in your own Google Drive account. We only store the metadata described above.
  • Google API Scopes: We request the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file scope. This means we can only access files that you create or open with our app. We do not have access to your entire Google Drive, and we cannot see files you haven't interacted with through MART Drive.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect solely to provide and improve the Service:

  • To authenticate you and grant you access to your MART Drive dashboard.
  • To display your file list and metadata (file names, sizes, etc.) so you can manage your files.
  • To perform file uploads and management actions directly within your Google Drive account via the Google Drive API.
  • To communicate with you regarding service updates, security alerts, or support requests (only if you contact us).

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Google API Services User Data Policy

Limited Use Disclosure: MART Drive's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

We only use the Google Drive API to provide the file management features you request. We do not use Google user data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to the Service. We do not transfer your Google data to third parties except as necessary to provide the Service (e.g., Firebase for authentication and metadata storage) or as required by law.

5. Data Security & Storage

The security of your data is important to us:

  • Firebase Firestore: We store file metadata (name, ID, size) in Firebase Firestore, a secure and scalable cloud database. Firebase is a Google product that implements industry-standard security measures.
  • Authentication: User authentication is handled securely by Google Firebase Authentication. We never store your Google password.
  • No Server-Side File Storage: We do not store the actual contents of your files on our servers. All file data resides in your Google Drive.
  • Data Retention: Metadata is retained while your account is active or as needed to provide the Service. You may revoke access at any time (see Section 6).

6. User Rights

You have control over your data:

  • Revoke Access: You can revoke MART Drive's access to your Google account at any time by visiting Google Account Permissions. Once revoked, you will no longer be able to use the Service until you grant access again.
  • Delete Metadata: If you wish to delete your MART Drive account and associated metadata, please contact us at the email below. We will remove your metadata from Firebase Firestore.
  • Access and Update: You can view and manage your files directly within the MART Drive app or through your Google Drive account.

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA) or other jurisdictions with data protection laws, you may have additional rights such as the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. Please contact us to exercise these rights.
